Home screen widget

Shows today's workout and your daily habits

1. Copy the widget code

2. Paste it into Scriptable

  1. Get Scriptable from the App Store (free).
  2. Open Scriptable and tap + in the top right.
  3. Tap the empty page and choose Paste.
  4. Tap Untitled Script at the top and rename it Lean Streak.
  5. Tap Done.

3. Add the widget

  1. Go to your home screen and press and hold an empty spot until the apps wiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (top left), then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Scriptable, pick the medium size (swipe to it), and tap Add Widget.
  4. Tap the new blank widget (still wiggling). For Script, choose Lean Streak.
  5. Tap outside the box, then Done. Drag it next to your Lean Streak app icon.

The widget updates itself each day. It can't show your streak or check-offs, because iPhone keeps Lean Streak's data private to the app, so log those in the app. Tapping the widget opens Scriptable, not Lean Streak.
